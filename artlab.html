Art Lab is a monthly subscription-based mail club, where active members receive a postcard print of artwork by yours truly each month. Read the launch announcement here.

How it works:

  1. Select a subscription below to check availability.
  2. If there's space for the month, you'll be able to register directly by completing the payment form via Stripe. If the month is full, leave your name and email to be added to the waitlist.
  3. Receive a piece of my artwork (and gratitude!) in the mail each month.
  4. Email artlabbyjocelyn@gmail.com anytime to update your address or to change your membership.

At the moment, Art Lab can only support delivery within the US.
