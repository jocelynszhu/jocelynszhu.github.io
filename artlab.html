Art Lab is a monthly subscription-based mail club, where active members receive a postcard print of artwork by yours truly each month.

How it works:

  1. Sign up to join the mailclub below.
  2. Requests to join will be processed in accordance with the month's availability. Once the request is approved, you will receive a payment link at the provided email.
  3. Email artlabbyjocelyn@gmail.com anytime to update your address or to change your membership.
  4. Receive a piece of my artwork (and gratitude!) in the mail each month.
