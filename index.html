I am a first-year PhD student in Public Policy (Judgment and Decision Making) at the Harvard Kennedy School. Before Harvard, I completed my B.S and M.Eng in Computation and Cognition at MIT, where I was fortunate to work with the Human Cooperation Lab and Consumer Attention Lab.

I study how digital technologies like social media and AI shape how people form beliefs, make judgments, and navigate an increasingly machine-mediated information environment.
