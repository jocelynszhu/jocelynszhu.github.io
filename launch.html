Art has always been a deeply joyful and life-giving experience for me. Over the past few years, I’ve experimented with many different styles and mediums, from oil painting and photography to collages and tattoos. But beyond personal satisfaction, I've recently been reflecting on how grateful I'd be for the chance to share my work with others.

I’m launching a new project I’m calling Art Lab, a subscription-based mail club where, for $6/month, I’ll send a postcard print of an art experiment directly to your door each month. The cost covers production, mailing, and materials, and will be updated as scaling allows. Postcards will be sent out on the 16th, so sign-ups will close on the 14th (provided availability does not run out before then).

You can sign up by navigating to my personal website here and filling out the form. You’ll see monthly and annual (10% off) membership levels as well as gift options if you’d like to support my art by sharing it with a friend! If you’d like to change your subscription status, email me any time at artlabbyjocelyn@gmail.com.

One last point: I’d love to invite you to join me. If you’re inclined to share something creative of your own, please email me for my address and mailing instructions. As both an artist and an academic researching behavior in digital environments, I’m always looking for ways to stay grounded in the physical and greatly encouraged by others doing the same.

I’m looking forward to starting this journey with you, and thank you so much for your support!

With gratitude and anticipation,
Jocelyn
